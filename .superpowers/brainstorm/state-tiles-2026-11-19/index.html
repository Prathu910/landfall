What changes, and what doesn't

  • Label: "Not yet verified" becomes "Rules being checked": it's about our check, not the user.
  • Look: a light card with a slate top line and a clock icon, instead of the dull grey fill. Slate is the brand's link and "Worth watching" colour, so it reads as "open, look closer", not "no".
  • Order: the published invitations lead, in the serif, as the tile's headline; the link says "See the evidence".
  • Same meaning: the group, its rules and the panel's "Not yet verified on the official page" lines stay exactly as they are. Red stays only for "Not open to you now".
  • Part A runs alongside: as I verify each state's rules on the official pages, tiles leave this group altogether (to "Needs your answers", "Worth watching" or "Strongest evidence", whatever the evidence says).
