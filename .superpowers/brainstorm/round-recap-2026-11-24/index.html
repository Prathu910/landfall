Monthly SkillSelect update: page and Instagram mockups

A look at what a monthly "SkillSelect update" could be: one page on the site and a set of Instagram images made from it. Nothing here is built into the real site yet.

  • Every number is real, taken from our October 2026 data (as at 31 Oct 2026) and the 15 Oct 2026 round. notes.md lists each one and where it came from.
  • For Instagram there are two looks to choose from for the first two images: APassport page (security-pattern paper, ink stamps, magazine-style headings) and BBig stamp (one huge stencil number, very little else).
  • Click any image to open it full size.

1. The update page proposed address: migratelens.com/updates/2026-10/

One page per month, in this order: a short dated answer at the top, the 189 round, 190 and 491 by state, biggest movers, how October compares with September, a "check your points" invitation, sources, and common questions. Open the working mockup (the round button in its header switches light and dark).

2. Instagram: pick a look first two images of the post

Same facts, two styles. The rest of the set below uses look A for now; it would switch to B if you prefer it.

APassport page

Feels like the site: a passport page with the fine wavy security pattern and ink stamps. Calmer, more to read.

BBig stamp

One huge stencil number that reads from across the room, then a single line. Louder, stops the scroll, less detail.

3. The rest of the post swipe order 3 to 6

A post is six images: cover, 189, 190 by state, 491 by state, one occupation up close, and a closing image with the link and the "not migration advice" line.

4. Stories tall format for Stories and Reels covers

Instagram puts its own buttons over the top and bottom of a story, so all the words sit in the middle.

5. Night versions the "passport at night" colours

For accounts or posts that want the darker look. Same facts as the day versions.

6. Things to decide

  • Look A or B for Instagram (or A for the post and B for stories)?
  • State numbers are minimums. The data hides every count under 20, so a state shows "at least 790". Honest, but a little odd to read. Keep it, or show only the state order (most to fewest)?
  • One page a month or one per round? Rounds are irregular (June, then October). The proposal is one page per data month, posted on round day with the round and filled in when the monthly data arrives.
  • Spotlight choice: rotate occupations each month, or let followers vote?

More detail, every number and the data cautions: notes.md.
