Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The check and your report are free. We ask for an email to show your report, and there's no account.

What do the labels mean?

They come from your occupation's last 3 listed 189 rounds. Strong means you meet every cut-off, Competitive the lowest one, Long wait none of them. Not currently invited means 189 hasn't listed your occupation in 4 rounds.

Are the counts people?

No, they count EOIs. One person can lodge several, and ties are broken by a date SkillSelect doesn't publish.

Does state nomination add points?

Yes: 5 for a 190 nomination and 15 for a 491. Each state also has its own rules, which your report checks separately.

Where do my answers go?

Your full profile stays in your browser. We keep only your email and a short summary: occupation, age band, onshore or offshore, and points.
