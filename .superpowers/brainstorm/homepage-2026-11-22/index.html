How we handle your data

A few things
worth knowing.

What exactly does MigrateLens do?+

It gives skilled migrants a free, personalised report for Australian PR planning: your points, an outlook for the 189, 190 and 491 pathways, and the published data behind the result.

Does a good result guarantee an invitation?+

No. Your outlook is an interpretation of published history. Invitation rounds, rules and your circumstances can change.

Are all three visas permanent?+

The 189 and 190 are permanent visas. The 491 is a provisional regional visa with a potential pathway to permanent residence, subject to the relevant requirements. Read the official 491 information.
