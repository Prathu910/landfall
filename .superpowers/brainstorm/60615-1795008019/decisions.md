# MigrateLens brand and homepage decisions (18 Nov 2026)

- **Brand direction: C · Data Desk** (`brand-directions.html`). Newsprint, ink and saffron, an editorial data-newsroom feel. A (Eucalypt & Gold) and B (Deep Field) were rejected.
- **Logo: C1 · Rising bars** (`data-desk-logo.html`). A lens ring holding three rising bars, with the tallest in saffron. C2 (your line) and C3 (wordmark only) were rejected. The founder raised no objection to the palette or status colours shown on the same screen ("Worth watching" moves from amber to slate).
- **Hero: combined** (`homepage-hero.html`, option C). A Stageshow animated demo in the hero, with a live real-data try-it directly below.
- **Positioning: P1 · Two doors** (`homepage-positioning.html`). The founder raised it: users who haven't lodged an EOI must be served too. The headline is "Know your points. See where they'd put you." with doors for "Not lodged an EOI yet" and "Already lodged", both leading to the same check. The wording is "points-test basics", never "eligibility".
- The founder had no reference sites in mind ("You are on your OWN"), so Claude chose the directions.
