The animated demo (A) sells the whole report in 12 seconds, including the state outlook, which a small widget can't show. Straight underneath, the live try-it (B) turns curiosity into a real number with no form to fill in, then hands off to the full check. Each does the job it's best at, and visitors who skip the animation still meet real data in the first scroll.

01HeroHeadline, two buttons, three trust points (A, B or C above).
