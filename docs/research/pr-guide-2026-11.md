## Points-test reform

- **Announced:** Budget Paper No. 2, 12 May 2026: "The points test will be optimised to select better educated, higher‑skilled and younger migrants overall." [bp2_2026-27.pdf](https://budget.gov.au/content/bp2/download/bp2_2026-27.pdf)
- **Said since:** the Minister for Home Affairs, National Press Club, 17 Sep 2026: "we have redone the points test in consultation with Treasury"; housing-trades skills to get the same points as a university qualification "on implementation". No start date or table. [Speech](https://minister.homeaffairs.gov.au/TonyBurke/Pages/speech-national-press-club-17092026.aspx)
- **Published:** the Home Affairs points table (last updated 4 Nov 2026) is still the current test. No replacement table or start date is published.
