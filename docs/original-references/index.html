Waiting is hard. Waiting blind is harder.

You lodge your EOI, and then the questions start. SkillSelect doesn't answer them, forums answer them ten different ways, and every round changes the picture.

How many people are actually ahead of me?

SkillSelect shows your points, not your place in the queue. Forum guesses range from "next round" to "never".

Why did my cut-off jump again?

In the same round, cut-offs ran from 65 to 100 points depending on the occupation. The headline number tells you almost nothing about yours.

Am I about to lose points without knowing?

A birthday, an English test turning three, an assessment expiring. Your score can quietly fall while you wait.

Which state would realistically pick me?

Eight states, eight sets of rules and lists, scattered across PDFs, announcements and Telegram groups.

Will my visa run out before an invitation arrives?

Your current visa's end date and your likely invitation window may not line up. Most people only check when it's urgent.

Would another 10 points even change anything?

Retakes and extra credentials cost money and months. Without your own queue numbers, it's a guess.

Questions people ask first.

Is this migration advice?

No. Your report shows what the official data says about your situation, with our estimates clearly labelled. We don't tell you what to do. For advice, speak to a registered migration agent or an Australian lawyer; your report gives them a clear starting point.

Where does the data come from?

Department of Home Affairs SkillSelect data, invitation round results, occupation ceilings and visa grant statistics, plus each state's published nomination information. Community-reported data is always marked as such.

How accurate are the estimates?

Your points and queue position come straight from official rules and data. Wait times and state chances are estimates, shown as ranges with a confidence level. We publish every prediction before each round and grade it afterwards.

Do you need my passport or documents?

No. We ask only for the details that affect your points and outlook, such as dates and test scores. Nothing is uploaded.

How quickly do I get the report?

Within minutes of paying. You can read it online straight away, and the PDF arrives by email shortly after.

What happens when a new round comes out?

For 3 months after purchase, your report refreshes automatically with new rounds and data, and shows you what changed since the last version.

What if my occupation isn't covered?

The free check tells you before you pay. If we still can't produce a full report for your occupation, you get your money back.

Is Landfall linked to the Australian Government?

No. We're independent. We use public Home Affairs data under its Creative Commons licence.
