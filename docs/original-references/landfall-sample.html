Glossary

Page 15
EOI
Expression of interest. Your profile in SkillSelect, which the government invites from.
Date of effect
When your points last changed. It breaks ties between people on the same points.
Invitation
The government's offer to apply for a visa. You then have 60 days to lodge.
Cut-off
The lowest points score invited for an occupation in a round.
Occupation ceiling
The most people in one occupation who can be invited in a program year.
ANZSCO
The six-digit code for your occupation, such as 261111.
Nomination
A state's backing for a 190 or 491 visa. It adds 5 or 15 points.
Pool
All submitted EOIs waiting for an invitation.
