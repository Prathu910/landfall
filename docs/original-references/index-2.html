Questions people ask first.

Is this migration advice?

No. Your report shows what official data says about your situation, with our estimates clearly labelled. For advice, speak to a registered migration agent or an Australian lawyer.

Where does the data come from?

Home Affairs SkillSelect data, invitation rounds, occupation ceilings and visa grant statistics, plus each state's published nomination information. Community-reported data is always marked.

How accurate is it?

Points and queue position come straight from official rules and data. Wait times and state chances are estimates, shown as ranges with a confidence level, and graded publicly after each round.

Do you need my passport or documents?

No. Only the details that affect your points and outlook, like dates and test scores. Nothing is uploaded.

What if my occupation isn't covered?

The free check tells you before you pay. If we still can't produce a full report, you get your money back.
