    English

    English is worth up to 20 points: 10 for Proficient, 20 for Superior. Competent English is required but earns no points.

    Which English test have you taken?

    A result counts only if the test was taken in the 3 years before you're invited. For PTE and TOEFL, the scores needed changed for tests taken from 7 August 2025, so the date sets which scale applies.

    Your score in each skill

    Copy each skill from your result. Your overall score doesn't count: every skill must reach the line.

    Check each skill against the official Home Affairs score table for your test.

    Education

    Only your highest qualification counts, but some Australian study earns extra points on top.

    Highest qualification

    For overseas study, use the level your skills assessment says it's comparable to.

    Do you meet the Australian study requirement?

    At least 2 academic years of CRICOS-registered study in Australia, completed over at least 16 months, taught in English, while you were in Australia.

    Worth 5 points, and it's needed before regional study points can count.

    Did you live and study in a designated regional area for that study?

    Major cities such as Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane don't count.

    Worth 5 more points.

    Do you have a specialist education qualification?

    A master's by research or doctorate from an Australian institution in an eligible STEM or ICT field, with at least 2 academic years of study.

    Worth 10 points. A coursework master's doesn't count here.

    Work experience

    Add each paid job in your nominated or a closely related occupation. We count it the way the points test does, so you don't have to work out years yourself.

    What counts as skilled employment
    • Paid work in your nominated or a closely related occupation, at least 20 hours a week.
    • Only within the 10 years before you're invited, and only after the date your assessment treats you as skilled.
    • Overseas: 3 years earns 5 points, 5 years 10, 8 years 15. In Australia: 1 year earns 5, 3 years 10, 5 years 15, 8 years 20.
    • Overseas and Australian points together are capped at 20.
    • We count calendar days including start and end dates. Home Affairs checks your evidence, so claims close to a threshold need care.

    Partner

    A few more details

    Two last ways to earn points, then details your report uses for queue and state comparisons.

    A credentialled community language (CCL) result, or a NAATI translator or interpreter credential.

    Worth 5 points while the credential is valid.
